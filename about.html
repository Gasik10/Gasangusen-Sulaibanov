About Me

I have recently completed my PhD at École Pratique des Hautes Études (EPHE), PSL, Paris (defended 30 March 2026), with a dissertation on the documentation and description of Tsugni Dargwa, a Caucasian language of Dagestan, Russia.

I am affiliated with PROCLAC – UMR 7192 «Proche-Orient – Caucase : langues, archéologie, cultures». See my researcher page on PROCLAC.

Principal research interests

  • Language description and documentation
  • East Caucasian (aka Nakh-Daghestanian) languages
  • Valency and morphosyntax
  • Grammatical semantics and grammaticalization
  • Linguistic typology
  • Language contact and areal linguistics
  • Ideophones and attributivisation
  • Cognitive linguistics
  • Linguistic fieldwork

I have attended international schools on language documentation and linguistics in France, Italy, the Netherlands, and Turkey.
