I am a linguist specializing in Dargwa (Dargic) languages of the East Caucasian (also known as Nakh-Daghestanian) language family, spoken in the Republic of Dagestan, Russia. I hold a PhD in Linguistics from the École Pratique des Hautes Études (EPHE), PSL, Paris. For my doctoral dissertation, I documented and described Tsugni Dargwa, a previously undocumented and undescribed language of the Dargic group within the East Caucasian language family.
